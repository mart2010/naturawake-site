# NaturaWake

**Wake up to a dawn, not an alarm.**

NaturaWake is an alarm clock for Android. Before your wake-up time, your smart
lamps brighten slowly from deep blue to warm gold while birdsong and water
sounds rise from silence. Then the alarm rings, if you need it to.

*Status: in development. Not on Google Play yet.*

## What it does

- A **dawn** of 5 to 60 minutes before your wake-up time: your lamps rise
  together with a natural soundscape.
- **Your own lamps.** Philips Hue (over Bluetooth, no bridge needed) and WiZ
  bulbs work today. With no lamp, it is a sound-only alarm.
- **The sound does not depend on the lamps or the network.** If a lamp cannot be
  reached, the dawn carries on without it.
- No account, no subscription: a free trial, then a one-time purchase.

## Why I made it

I always thought it would be easier to wake up in a more natural setting than
with an alarm, and that light was the obvious companion. Many years later my
daughter asked me to buy her a sunrise alarm clock. I joked that they had stolen
my old idea, and decided to build it for her instead.

NaturaWake is that idea, using the lamps you already own and the phone you
already carry.
