## A dawn that feels natural

**The light.** A real dawn does not switch on. It begins in deep blue and
violet, turns rose, then soft amber, and ends in a gentle gold, getting brighter
all along. NaturaWake walks your lamps through those colours, slowly, over a
dawn of 5 to 60 minutes (20 to 30 is about what nature takes). You choose when
you want to be awake; the dawn is timed to end then.

**The sound.** No synthetic beeps. The ambience is made of recordings of the
real thing: gentle rain, a quiet stream with birds, leaves in the wind, a forest,
songbirds. It starts almost silent and rises smoothly, so you surface from sleep
instead of being jolted out of it.

**Both together.** Light and sound rise on the same curve, so the room slowly
brightens and fills with life.

## What it does

- Works with the lamps you already own: Philips Hue (over Bluetooth, no bridge
  needed) and WiZ bulbs today. With no lamp, it is a sound-only alarm.
- **The sound does not depend on the lamps or the network.** If a lamp cannot be
  reached, the dawn carries on without it.
- Several alarms, repeat days, and a dismiss button at any time.
- No account, no subscription: a free trial, then a one-time purchase.

## Why I made it

I always thought it would be easier to wake up in a more natural setting than
with an alarm, and that light was the obvious companion. Many years later my
daughter asked me to buy her a sunrise alarm clock. I joked that they had stolen
my old idea, and decided to build it for her instead.

NaturaWake is that idea, using the lamps you already own and the phone you
already carry.
